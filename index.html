The Lore

♥

One lazy afternoon in heaven, Moe the cupid was busy lounging around when she accidentally knocked over the Book of Fates. Papers were everywhere! And in her panic she shoved them back in any order... Accidentally scrambling the soulmates. Whoops!

Now people who were never supposed to cross paths are now inexplicably tied together. Enemies? Soulmates. Rivals? Lovers. Complete opposites? Well, let's see how that goes.

So why MixsedMatch? It’s a mash-up of Mixed and Missed: soulmates that got mixed up, and matches fate completely missed.

If fate says they shouldn't be together, that's probably exactly what we're looking for.

The open collab is for Janitor AI creators who want to play with those scrambled pairings. Make a bot with an “opposites attract” trope where the couple was never meant to work, but did. Tag it #MixsedMatch and swipe through the matches below.

Note: To be featured on the site, a bot must use the tag, include the banner in its source code, and be promoted in Velvet Hour.

“Opposites Attract”

♥

Here, Opposites Attract is an umbrella term. It doesn’t only mean “the quiet one and the loud one.” It covers any pairing where the two people are on different sides of something, and fate put them together anyway.

Every bot in the collab is Opposites Attract, so when you submit, use your “main trope” spot for the specific dynamic (like Enemies to lovers) instead.

Some examples of what fits under it:

  • Enemies to lovers
  • Rivals to lovers
  • Fake dating
  • Forced proximity
  • Grumpy x Sunshine
  • Introvert x Extrovert

Other ideas: Rich x Poor, Villain x Hero, Troublemaker x Model student, Paparazzi x Celebrity, Police x Criminal, Human x Ghost/Supernatural, Werewolf x Vampire, Player x Virgin, Popular x Nerd, Emo x Bimbo, Royalty x Civilian, Exes/ Toxic Exes, Con artist x Naive, Cynical x Optimistic, Fire x Water, Emotional x Stoic, Tomboy x Girly, Smart x Airhead, Professor x Student, and so many more!

That’s not the full list, so if your pair were never meant to work, they probably belong here.

How do I join?

♥

No lorebook, no complicated requirements, and no need to join a server to take part! This is an open collab, and everyone is welcome to participate.

The main theme is Opposites Attract.

Posting is open from February 14th - August 14th 2027.

All bots must use the tag #MixsedMatch (spelled M-I-X-S-E-D, and the exact spelling matters or your bot won’t show up in the tag search).
